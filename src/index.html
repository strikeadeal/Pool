    How to play

    1. Aim: drag anywhere on the table to turn the cue. Tap a spot to point straight at it.
    2. Fine-tune: slide the ribbed roller for tiny adjustments.
    3. Shoot: pull the power bar down, then let go. Slide back to the top to cancel.
    4. Spin: tap the little cue ball to hit above, below or to the side of centre.

    8-ball rules

    • Break from behind the line. Pot a ball or send four to a cushion, or it’s an illegal break.
    • The table is open after the break. The first ball you pot legally makes that group (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15) yours.
    • Hit one of your own balls first, then pot a ball or make any ball touch a cushion. Pot one of yours to keep shooting.
    • Fouls give your opponent ball in hand: they can place the cue ball anywhere. Potted balls stay down.
    • Clear your group, then pot the 8 in the pocket you call (the game suggests one; tap a pocket to change it). Choose Any pocket when starting a game to drop the call.
    • You lose if you pot the 8 early, in the wrong pocket, or on a foul.

    Tap the screen during a shot to fast-forward.
